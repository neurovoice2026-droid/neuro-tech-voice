# ElevenLabs feature audit (October 2026)

Every capability of the official ElevenLabs API that matters to an AI phone
receptionist was compared with this code base: 365 capabilities across the
Agents Platform (`/v1/convai/*`), voices, TTS, pronunciation, workspace
webhooks/secrets/subscription. Source of truth: the official OpenAPI spec
(`https://api.elevenlabs.io/openapi.json`, 407 operations, 190 under
`/v1/convai`) and the official docs. Each area was inventoried, then
adversarially re-verified against the spec and the code, then a completeness
check walked every operation and every Create Agent field.

Status counts: 145 implemented, 20 partial, 87 deferred, 113 not applicable / already correct.

- **Implemented / Partial** — shipped in this release; per-slice details,
  migrations, env vars and staging checks are in `docs/elevenlabs/<slice>.md`
  (A1 conversation behaviour, A2 platform settings & rollout, B1 tool
  infrastructure, B2 in-call business tools, C telephony, D conversations &
  analytics, E knowledge base, F voices, G browser tests & quota monitoring,
  H account deletion).
- **Not applicable / already correct** — either already used correctly, or a
  product the receptionist does not need (dubbing, music, studio, …).
- **Deferred** — useful but intentionally not shipped now; the reason and the
  risk are in the notes (e.g. outbound campaigns need a legal framework,
  WhatsApp needs a Meta business account, branches/deployments need a release
  process).

Not covered on purpose (other ElevenLabs products): dubbing, studio/projects,
music, sound effects, audio isolation, speech-to-speech, video/image flows,
audio-native, productions. They are not part of a phone receptionist.
