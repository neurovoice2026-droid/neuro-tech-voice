## Method (`trailer/out/ig/qa/ig5/voices/`, kept; `out/` is ignored)

- **`analyse.mjs`** is the ig1–ig4 / film 2 measuring tape (`out/kb/voices/analyse.mjs`) re-pointed at ig5's four sets. It
  measures rate, pauses at punctuation (audio silence and aligned gap), YIN F0 (range, movement, fry), question rises and
  statement landings, key-phrase emphasis (several keys per line), clicks / spikes / drops / roughness, peak and knee zone,
  and duration against the SCRIPT §5.1 budgets. It adds word-timing sanity (n/n, monotonic, shared onsets, onsets in
  silence, phrase-initial drift) and the SII-style score of every word against the placeholder mix's ducked bed (at the mix
  level and +6 dB). It runs a copy of the real `timing.ts` on every one-set cast, on the picks and on every one-line swap of
  the picks (`--picks=picks.json`), and prints a line's F0 track with `--track=<id>`.
  - `report-1.txt` / `report-picks.txt` / `metrics.json` are the runs before the install; `report-post.txt` /
    `metrics-post.json` re-ran the sims on the re-anchored `timing.ts`.
- **`search.mjs`** searches all 16,384 one-take-per-role casts with a replica of `layout()` (now the re-anchored one).
- **`swaps.mjs`** checks the alternates as placed and the forced trim rungs.
- **`compare.mjs`** compares the old and the re-anchored stop-time over 262,144 casts.
- **`sync.mjs`** is the end-to-end sync check against the built stems.
