## 2. Verdict

**Winner: Phone, "Off the hook".** 8.0 as drafted, about 8.5 with the grafts in §4.

**Why it wins on growth:**
1. **It is the only concept that is in the receptionist family on day 1.** "receptionist" is heard at ≈ 0.4 s. The other three need a second post (the B hook) to get there.
2. **The magic beat sits where TikTok decides.** The handset lifts by itself at 4.5 s ("who picked up?"). The other pickups land at 7.5 s (free), 12.0 s (clock) and 15.5 s (sign).
3. **Its share is a send, not a save.** Front-desk staff and owner-operators tag each other. The clock's share is a design admiration share, which is how the reference itself travelled: among motion designers, not shop owners (RESEARCH-viral §2.2).
4. **The payoff makes people replay.** The pun lands on the reveal frame, which is also the screenshot.
5. **It still delivers the owner's "exactly in this style".** White ground, one object in macro, black plus teal, Instrument Sans plus Cormorant italic, two tiers, match cuts. Its P5 crop (b7) is the reference composition with our object: the dial arc of white-centred finger holes and a black finger stop as the hand.

**Runner-up, and the next reel: the clock.** It has the best body (loop, sweep, tick-to-glide) and is the literal reference look the owner pointed at. Its weakness is the day-1 hook and the 12.5 s keyword, not the build. Post it as **ig7** a week or two after ig6, with its own receptionist hook on day 1: "One ring, six seconds. Your receptionist can't answer." (84.4 STRONG raw and patched). Projected 7.0 with that hook.

**What the winner carries into the other judges' hands:**
- **Takeover reading (U5).** A desk phone answering itself can read as "the AI answers my existing line". The draft's mitigation is the ig1/ig3 precedent: caption and pinned comment say "its own number, next to your line" (`docs/ig/SCRIPT.md` §0.4). Growth adds a ready reply (§8). The truth judge decides.
- **"First ring" is grade B.** "Ring it yourself." invites viewers to test it, so a second ring will be reported in public. The clock's launch check is grafted as G5.

## 6. The growth read, concept by concept

### Phone "Off the hook" (winner)
- **Keep:** the hook and "Neither can you."; the lift before any second ring with the cord turning teal; the three-pickup montage on the beat (the satisfying layer, and concrete times owners recognise); the reveal pun; the hang-up-and-ring-again seam; the cover "Your receptionist / *can't be* / *in two places.*", which sits next to ig1's "Don't fire your receptionist." on the grid.
- **Fix:** G1 (keyword in big type), G2 (stay on the handset in the 3-s window), G3 (cut the pre-payoff explainer, 26 s).
- **TikTok risk:** "How does it get my calls?" Reply ready (§8). Retro rotary phone may read as old-fashioned to some viewers; the ☎ silhouette is also the most recognisable phone shape there is, and the mock reads at thumbnail size.

### Clock "Every second" (runner-up, ig7)
- **Best parts:** the most beautiful frame and the closest to the owner's reference; the honest six-second demonstration; the best loop; "Ring it yourself." after a transformation you can hear.
- **Weak for growth:** the frame-0 card has no stake (45.2); the opening asks muted viewers for patience; product at 12.0 s, keyword at 12.5 s; "You can't answer." repeats ig5's "You can't.".
- **A risk nobody listed [K]:** a new account's video is usually first tested on viewers near the poster, and the owner posts from Romania. The six-second ring is North American only (ETSI is 1 s on, 4 s off; the UK differs, RESEARCH-viral F1), so many first-pool viewers will not recognise "their" ring. The pinned reply handles corrections, but the "you feel the six seconds" effect is weaker for them.
- **For ig7:** day-1 hook "One ring, six seconds. Your receptionist can't answer." (84.4). It moves "receptionist" to ≈ 1.5 s.

### Sign "The other side"
- **Best part:** the strongest muted frame 0 of the four (81.4 with the sign's own word) and a real "huh, that's true" twist.
- **Weak for growth:** the after-hours family is our floor (ig2 250) and the house line opened the account at 52 views; keyword at 16.5 s; pickup at 15.5 s; little to comment on. Its B hook scores 65.2 raw. Not grafted: its edge depends on an object carrying a word, which the phone cannot copy without unspoken text.

### Free "While you were out"
- **Best parts:** the lowest truth risk, the most concrete product mechanics, the best caption question and reply bank (grafted as G4).
- **Weak for growth:** the weakest frame 0 (small pad, tiny labels); an empty-fields macro in the 3-s window; the lift at 7.5 s; "Three calls." next to ig5's "Three rings."; its receptionist B hook is 10 words.

## 9. Measurement plan

**Post on TikTok first** (the 60 fps copy, the custom cover if the app offers it), then Instagram the same day.

**At about 48 h, read TikTok analytics:** the retention curve, average watch time and the share who watched the full video. Read the curve at four points:
1. **≈ 2.7 s** (the G2 punch-in): a cliff here means the hook card, not the body.
2. **≈ 4.5 s** (the lift): the curve should flatten here; if it doesn't, the magic beat isn't reading.
3. **≈ 8-15 s** (montage and "from your words"): the explanation stretch.
4. **≈ 17.6 s** (the payoff) and the end: the full-watch share is the completion signal TikTok weights [P1].

**Decisions:**
- **Steep drop before 3 s:** a week later, post the same body as a separate TikTok with `ig6-01b` "Two places at once? Your receptionist can't." (84.4).
- **Holds past the lift but sags at 8-15 s:** the next reel (ig7, the clock) keeps its middle shorter.
- **Instagram:** Trial Reels are probably still locked at 14 followers (eligibility reports range from "everyone" to ≥ 200, RESEARCH-reels §3.9). Check the composer toggle; if it is there, A/B the hook there instead.

**What counts:** AGENT comments per platform, Linktree clicks on TikTok, trial sign-ups by UTM (`tt_ig6` against `reel_ig6`), agents created and test calls made. Five reels are too few to call a hook family from views alone; ig6 is the first deliberate second test of the receptionist family, so log it in `log.md` with its hook formula.
